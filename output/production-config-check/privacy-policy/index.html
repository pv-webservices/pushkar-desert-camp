Privacy & Enquiries

This page describes the features of this website and the direct enquiry process.

Information entered in the enquiry form

The form prepares a message in your browser using the details you enter. It does not submit the information to a website database or send a message automatically. Opening the WhatsApp link takes you to WhatsApp, where you can review and choose to send your enquiry.

Direct contact services

Calling, emailing or messaging the business shares the information you choose to provide through your phone, email service or WhatsApp. Those services may apply their own privacy policies. Please share only information relevant to your visit.

Website storage

The website code does not set tracking cookies, use analytics or save enquiry details in local storage. Your hosting provider may process technical access logs according to its own configuration.

Booking enquiries

An enquiry is a request for information, rather than a confirmed reservation. Availability, prices, timings, inclusions, payment arrangements and cancellation conditions must be agreed directly with the business before you book.

Privacy questions

For questions about information you have shared with Pushkar Desert Safari, contact info@pushkardesertcamp.com or call +91 9116991219.
