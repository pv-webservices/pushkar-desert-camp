A few helpful details

Before You Visit

We’ll help you plan the experience that suits your visit. Contact us for current details and availability.

Are performances available every evening?

Performance dates, timings and arrangements are confirmed directly with the team. Please check what is available for your preferred visit.

Can I enquire about a cultural evening for a group?

Send your date and group size, and let the team know you are interested in folk dance or fire performances. They will discuss the available arrangements.

Are cultural performances included in a stay?

Please ask the team about the inclusions of your particular stay or visit. No performance inclusion is guaranteed by this website.
