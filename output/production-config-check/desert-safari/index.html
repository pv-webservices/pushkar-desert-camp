A few helpful details

Before You Visit

We’ll help you plan the experience that suits your visit. Contact us for current details and availability.

How do I arrange a safari?

Call or send a WhatsApp enquiry with your preferred date and group size. The team will share availability and the current safari details.

What is the duration, route and price?

Contact us to confirm the current route, duration, price and inclusions. These details are agreed directly with the team before your visit.

Can I combine a safari with a camp stay?

Enquire about both experiences together. The team can discuss the available arrangements for your preferred dates.

What should I check before visiting?

Ask the team about suitable clothing, weather conditions, participation requirements and any arrangements needed for your group.
